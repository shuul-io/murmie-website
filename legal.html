Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Murmie app and this website handle. The app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. The website uses Microsoft Clarity for analytics (see "This website" below).

Data the app keeps on your Mac

  • Audio. While you record, audio is written to a temporary file. The file is deleted when the recording stops, and any leftover files are removed the next time the app starts. Audio is transcribed on your Mac.
  • History. Your transcripts are kept in a local history. History is on by default. It keeps up to 500 entries and deletes entries older than 2 weeks (you can choose 1 to 4 weeks). You can turn history off or clear it in settings.
  • Meeting notes. Meeting transcripts and summaries are kept until you delete them. If you turn on export, they are also saved to a folder you choose.
  • Settings. Stored in the standard macOS preferences file.
  • API keys. Stored in your macOS Keychain.
  • Clipboard. To paste text, the app briefly puts it on your clipboard, then restores what was there. Ask AI answers are copied to your clipboard and stay there.

Murmie does not encrypt history or meeting notes itself. They may be included in your Mac's backups.

Data that leaves your Mac

  • Model downloads. Speech and AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face when you set them up. If you choose Apple's speech engine, macOS downloads its language files from Apple. These providers see your IP address and what you download. No audio or text is sent.
  • Update checks. About once a day, Murmie asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and it downloads updates from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address and the version of Murmie that is asking. No audio or text is sent. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings › About.
  • Feedback form. If you send feedback from Settings › About, the form opens in your browser and what you type is stored with Google Forms. Please do not include personal or confidential information. We use it only to fix problems and decide what to build.
  • AI services you connect. These are off by default. If you turn on AI correction, Ask AI or meeting summaries with a cloud AI service or custom endpoint, the app sends that service:
    • AI correction: your transcribed text;
    • Ask AI: your spoken request as text, any text you have selected, and a screenshot of a window or screen if you pick one;
    • meeting summaries: the meeting transcript.
    Audio is never sent. If you use an on-device model or an AI server on your own Mac, nothing leaves your Mac.

These providers may process data outside your country, often in the United States. We receive no data from the app, so we do not sell or share it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your voice.
  • Speech Recognition: only if you choose Apple's speech engine.
  • Accessibility: to insert text at your cursor and, for Ask AI, read selected text.
  • Input Monitoring: to detect your shortcut, such as a double-tap of Right ⌘.
  • Automation: to paste into apps such as Terminal.
  • Screen and System Audio Recording: to capture system audio for meeting notes and the window you pick for Ask AI.

You can turn off any permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and downloads come from GitHub. GitHub processes your IP address to serve the pages and downloads; see GitHub's privacy statement. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the website. Clarity loads a script from clarity.ms and records how you interact with the pages, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, and can replay a visit as a recording. Microsoft receives your IP address and browser details with these requests and processes the data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We use no other analytics or third-party scripts and show no ads. Fonts are served from murmie.com.

Your choices

Everything the app stores is on your Mac, so you can view and delete it yourself. We hold no copy. Deleting the app leaves its data behind. To remove the data Murmie created, quit the app, then also delete:

  • ~/Library/Preferences/com.murmie.app.plist (settings, history and meeting notes);
  • the Murmie and FluidAudio folders in ~/Library/Application Support;
  • the Whisper models in ~/Documents/huggingface/models, or the folder you chose in settings (other apps may share this folder);
  • the com.murmie.app items in Keychain Access;
  • any meeting notes you exported.

If you are in the EU or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies

murmie.com itself sets no cookies. If you use the theme toggle, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage as murmie-theme. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Microsoft Clarity, our analytics provider, may set these cookies in your browser:

  • _clck and _clsk: a visitor ID, and a link between your page views in one visit.
  • CLID, MUID, SM, MR and ANONCHK: set by Microsoft to recognise your browser across its sites.

Microsoft says Clarity sets no cookies for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland until it receives a consent signal. This website sends none. You can block Clarity with a content blocker or your browser's tracking protection. Clarity does not respond to Do Not Track signals.
