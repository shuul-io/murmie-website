Murmie

Just say it. Murmie types it.

Private, on-device voice dictation for macOS. Double-tap Right ⌘, speak, and your words appear wherever you're typing.

How to use it

  • Right Command, twice ⌘right ⌘right Start dictating
  • Right Command, once ⌘right Finish pastes at the cursor
  • Escape esc Cancel while recording
  • Hold Right Option ⌥right hold Push-to-talk optional
